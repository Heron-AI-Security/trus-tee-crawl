The loop around them is ops/attested/verify_time_export.py. It follows verify/_common.py time_evidence() of the same commit line by line, with one deliberate difference: a crawl without a challenge nonce has nonce null in every record, and time_evidence() compares the nonce with a file of the run, so it would refuse every record. Here a record is tied to the run by its task name (it must lie in the files of its own task), by one nonce value everywhere, and by the receipt hashes that its end reading commits to.
